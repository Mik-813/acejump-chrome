# AceJump for Chrome: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-27_

AceJump does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or browsing data.

## What the extension accesses

- **Page content:** when you trigger AceJump (keyboard shortcut or toolbar icon), it reads the visible text of the current tab locally in your browser to find and label matches. This text never leaves your browser and is not stored.
- **Settings:** your preferences (theme, label characters, custom CSS, behaviour options) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google syncs them between your browsers.
- **Last query (optional):** if "remember last query" is enabled, your last search text is saved locally with `chrome.storage.local` on your device only.

## What the extension does not do

- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- It does not access any tab unless you explicitly trigger it there.
- It does not share data with third parties.
